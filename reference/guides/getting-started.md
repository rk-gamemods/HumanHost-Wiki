# Getting started

Your first hours: what to gather, which bench to build first, and the tools and weapons you can make from the starting area.

## Gather these first

Break rocks, trees and rubble with a melee weapon or your bare hands. With a gun or bow out, nothing drops.

Everything below can be had in the first biome, [Mountain Forest](https://rk-gamemods.github.io/HumanHost-Wiki-Biomes/entry/e-93c8ff19b1c828559fed5df2bc2bb24c/?snapshot=build-25752290-072c9ca90af0&release=6207400cdf3a24ef64685d9319cc4471bbf1b314d8bbb3a0d5fb732fd8e3ec72). Most things in Human Host can also turn up in crates and on bodies anywhere, so this list is what you can count on, not all you might find.
