# Progression by biome

What each biome adds: new raw materials, what they let you craft, and what to build before you move on.

## How the world is laid out

The world is a set of rings around your starting point, one biome per ring. The game numbers the biomes 1 to 10 going outward. Each is about 2 km wide, and the pattern repeats after biome 10.

Close to the spawn point there are patches of mixed ground where any ore can turn up, but only about 1% of dig hits find one. Each ore is far more common in its home biome, listed below.
